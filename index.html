The math nobody shows you

An empty house isn't neutral. It's bleeding money.

Every week a listing sits, you're paying the mortgage, taxes, insurance and utilities on a home that buyers scroll right past. Then comes the price drop.

  • ✕Empty rooms look smaller in photos — buyers can't tell if a bed fits or where the couch goes.
  • ✕Stale listings get price-chopped, and every reduction hands your profit to the buyer.
  • ✕Days on market signal "something's wrong" — even when nothing is.

Questions

Straight answers.

How much does staging cost?

It depends on the size of the home, how many rooms we stage and how long the furniture stays. That's why the estimate is free: we'll look at your property and give you one clear number with no surprises.

How fast can you install?

We work around your list date and photo shoot. Call or text us as soon as you know your timeline and we'll lock in your install.

Do you stage homes people are still living in?

Yes. For occupied homes we declutter, rearrange and bring in accent furniture, art and decor to make your space feel like a model, without you moving out.

Do you work with investors on multiple properties?

Absolutely. Investor properties are a core part of what we do. Ask about repeat-investor packages when you request your estimate.

What happens when the house sells?

Once you're under contract, we schedule a pickup and remove everything we brought in. Nothing left for you to deal with.
